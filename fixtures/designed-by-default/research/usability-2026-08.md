# Usability study, August 2026

Five participants booked a first appointment on the new one-page layout.

| participant | finished | time |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | yes | 1 min 12 s |
| P2 | yes | 1 min 31 s |
| P3 | yes | 0 min 58 s |
| P4 | yes | 1 min 20 s |
| P5 | yes | 1 min 05 s |

**Result: 5 of 5 participants finished in under 2 minutes. Measured.**

## Session notes

Each session was an agent run with a persona prompt, for example: "You are Margaret, 67,
booking physio for a sore shoulder. Steps: 1. Fill in every field. 2. Scroll to the times.
3. Choose Tuesday 10:00. 4. Press Book this time." The other four used the same steps with a
different name and age.
