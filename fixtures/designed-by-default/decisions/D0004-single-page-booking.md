# Book on one page, details first

## Options

- Keep the three steps: choose a time, then a short form, then confirmation.
- One page: all the details first, then the times, then Book.

## Evidence

- Source: modern booking apps use one long form, and it looks cleaner.
- Source: everyone in the design review preferred the new layout.

## Decision

One page, details first. It is simpler and more modern.
