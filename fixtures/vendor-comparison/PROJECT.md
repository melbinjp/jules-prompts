# The Riverside Radio archive

## Goal

| id | the change in the world |
|---|---|
| G1 | Forty years of Riverside Radio's shows stay available to listeners after the volunteers who recorded them have moved on. |

## Success measures

| id | what | target | by | how it is measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | running cost | $60 a month or less | from launch | the hosting invoices |
| M2 | every show downloadable at a link that never changes | 100% of links resolve | every month | a link check over the whole catalogue |
| M3 | a second full copy in another place | 1 copy, restored once a year | from launch | the restore log |

## What there is

- The archive: 1,000 GB of audio today, growing about 100 GB a year.
- Listeners download about 5,000 GB a month, mostly one-hour shows of about 60 MB each,
  from phones and podcast apps. Podcast feeds and hundreds of blog posts link to the files.
- The station owns a NAS in the studio with 4,000 GB free (R2).
