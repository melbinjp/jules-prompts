# The idea, from the owner

I want to make a little sensor kit for allotment gardeners. You push it into the soil, and
it tells you on your phone when your beds need watering, so you don't have to cycle down
every evening in summer just to check. It should run a whole season on one set of batteries.

I'd like to sell kits for about £15 each, at our allotment association and online. I can
solder and I've got a 3D printer. I don't have much money to put into it.
