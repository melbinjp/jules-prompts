# Tidewise 1.0

- See the next safe paddling window for any beach or slipway, from the tide tables and the
  wind forecast, even with no signal once the week is downloaded.
- Tidal streams for 42 crossings around Wales and the South West, with slack water marked.
- Plan a trip, share it with your group, and get a reminder an hour before you need to launch.

Tidewise gives times and heights from the official tables and a forecast. It does not replace
local knowledge, a chart, or the coastguard's advice. Tell us what is wrong at help@tidewise.app.
