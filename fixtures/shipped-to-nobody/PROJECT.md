# Tidewise

## Goal

| id | the change in the world |
|---|---|
| G1 | Sea kayakers in the UK plan trips around tides with a phone app they trust, instead of three websites and a printed table. |

## Success measures

| id | what | target | by | how it is measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | people who install and plan a first trip | 200 | 2026-11-30 | first-trip events, counted on the device and sent only with consent |
| M2 | people who plan a second trip within 30 days | 40% of M1 | 2026-12-31 | the same events |
| M3 | help messages answered within one working day | 95% | from release | the help inbox |

## Who it is for

Sea kayakers, mostly in clubs. Evidence: 31 conversations at four clubs in spring 2026; the
UK Sea Kayaking Forum's trip-planning board (about 600 posts a month); the club newsletters of
the 14 clubs in the regional association. They talk about tidal streams, slack water and
crossings; none of the 31 asked what the app is written in.

## Reach

| route | who | measure |
|---|---|---|
| the forum's trip-planning board | kayakers planning trips now | installs with `?ref=forum` |
| club newsletters, through the 14 club secretaries | club members | installs with `?ref=clubs` |
| the paddling shops in Anglesey and Pembrokeshire | people buying charts | installs with `?ref=shops` |

## Change course

The goal stands. When a condition is measured, the project switches to the route it names.

| id | condition | then |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | fewer than 50 installs in the first two weeks | send the club secretaries the one-page guide and a short demo, and ask the two paddling shops to stock the QR card |
| K2 | fewer than 20% of people who install plan a first trip | watch five first-time kayakers set it up, and fix what stops them |
